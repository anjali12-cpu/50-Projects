# Retention Playbook (Demo)

## Start with a respectful conversation

Ask what the customer needs and listen before recommending an action. Do not assume that a high-risk score means the customer intends to leave. Confirm account details through approved company systems before making account changes.

## Review service friction

Repeated support contacts can signal unresolved friction. Review the recent support history, identify whether the same issue came back, and route unresolved technical problems to the appropriate support team. Explain the next step and an expected update time.

## Match the offer to the situation

If price is a concern, explain available plan options and their terms clearly. If usage is low, ask whether the product still fits the customer's needs before recommending a change. Do not promise discounts or benefits that are not approved.

## Protect customer information

Use only the minimum customer information needed for the task. Do not paste real personal or payment information into this demo or an unapproved model. Follow the organization's retention, privacy, and escalation rules.

## Interpret model scores cautiously

A churn probability is a model estimate, not a fact or a reason to treat a customer differently without review. Check data quality, consider the model's limitations, and use a human decision-maker for any customer-impacting action.
